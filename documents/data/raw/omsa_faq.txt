Frequently Asked Questions

Can students complete OMSA while working full time?

Yes. Many students complete the program while maintaining full-time employment. Advisors often recommend starting with one course to understand the workload.

How many courses should new students take?

New students are generally encouraged to begin with a lighter course load. Course difficulty varies significantly, and time requirements differ across courses.

What technical skills are helpful?

Programming experience, especially in Python, is helpful. Knowledge of probability, statistics, and linear algebra can also improve student success.

How long does the program take?

Completion time varies depending on course load. Many students finish in two to four years.

What resources are available?

Students have access to academic support, advising resources, online discussion forums, and course-specific instructional staff.
