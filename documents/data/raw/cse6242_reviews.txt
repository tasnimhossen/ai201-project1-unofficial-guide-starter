Review 1:
CSE 6242 Data and Visual Analytics is frequently described as one of the most demanding OMSA courses. Students mention large team projects, challenging assignments, and significant time commitments. Many reviews describe the workload as heavy. Several students recommend taking this course with lighter electives because project deadlines can require many hours of work each week.

Review 2:
Students often comment that the course teaches valuable real-world analytics skills. Team projects require communication, planning, and technical implementation. While the workload can be frustrating, many graduates report using the skills learned in professional environments after completing the program.

Review 3:
A common recommendation is to begin assignments early. Students who procrastinate often struggle with deadlines because projects involve multiple components. Reviews frequently emphasize that organization and time management are critical for success.

Review 4:
Many students believe the course is worthwhile despite the difficulty. Topics such as data visualization, analytics workflows, and collaborative project development are repeatedly mentioned in reviews. Students often leave the course with a stronger portfolio of practical work.

Review 5:
Several reviews note that teammates can strongly influence the experience. Strong groups often report positive outcomes while weaker teams may encounter communication challenges. Students consistently recommend maintaining regular contact with project partners.
