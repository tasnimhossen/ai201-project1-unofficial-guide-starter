The OMSA curriculum consists of foundational courses, advanced analytics courses, and electives. Students are expected to develop skills in programming, statistical modeling, optimization, machine learning, and data visualization.

Foundational coursework introduces analytics concepts and computational tools. These courses establish the knowledge required for later advanced coursework.

Advanced courses allow students to deepen their understanding of machine learning, simulation, optimization, and large-scale analytics.

Students complete elective coursework based on personal interests and career goals. Popular electives include deep learning, simulation, and machine learning-related topics.

The curriculum is designed to balance theoretical knowledge with practical analytical skills. Many assignments involve applying concepts to realistic datasets and decision-making problems.
